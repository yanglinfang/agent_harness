# ADR 0000: ADR Template

## Status

Template. Copy this file as `NNNN-short-slug.md` and fill in.

## Context

What is the problem being addressed? What forces are at play (technical, organizational, contractual)? Be specific. Cite prior discussion or rounds where relevant.

## Decision

The decision in one or two sentences, written in active voice ("We will use X").

Then expand:

- What exactly we are doing
- What is in scope
- What is explicitly out of scope

## Alternatives Considered

For each alternative:

- **Name**: short description
- **Pros**: why it was attractive
- **Cons**: why we rejected it

Include "do nothing" or "defer" as an alternative when relevant.

## Consequences

- **Positive**: what becomes easier
- **Negative**: what becomes harder, what debt we accept
- **Neutral**: things to be aware of

## References

- Round / discussion threads
- External docs, RFCs, prior art
- Links to related ADRs
